Grav Helios Converters Beta

Open education and publishing tools for the Grav Premium Helios Theme

Your content. Your call. The Grav Helios Course Hub and Open Reader give your course or open content a modern, clean home, designed to be an enjoyable experience for the students or readers who'll use it. Built with Grav CMS for educators and open publishers, they're ready-to-run skeleton packages that you keep and control.

These converters turn your LMS course content or Pressbooks book content into a website using the open-source Grav CMS: future-friendly Markdown files, no database, self-hosted, and each page individually embeddable back into Canvas, Moodle, or almost any LMS via a URL parameter. That same open format also makes it easy for open publishers and OER-minded educators to keep their content publicly accessible and ready for others to reuse.

NEW
Standard Markdown — both converters now offer an option to skip Helios-specific shortcodes entirely, producing plain Markdown that also works on other Markdown-based platforms, not just Grav Helios Course Hub or Open Reader. Look for the toggle in each converter's settings.

Don't have a Grav Helios site yet? The default, Helios-flavored output requires an existing Grav Helios Course Hub or Grav Helios Open Reader installation to use the converted files – both already include demo content, so you'll see a working example right away. (The Standard Markdown option above needs no Grav installation at all.) Or convert your own content now and preview the Markdown output first, no installation needed.

Common Cartridge Converter Helios Course Hub

Canvas · Moodle · Brightspace · Blackboard → Grav Helios Course Hub

Convert a Common Cartridge (.imscc) export into a modern course home, yours to keep: embed individual pages into your LMS, or run it standalone and partner with other platforms for grading, assignments, and quizzes. Modules, learning objectives, embedded video, course image, license, and syllabus all carried across.

Pressbooks Converter Helios Open Reader

Pressbooks XHTML export → Grav Helios Open Reader

Convert a Pressbooks XHTML export into a better reading experience, built for the web. Sections, chapters, learning objectives, H5P activity links, cover image, license, and OER attribution all carried across automatically. Shareable, searchable, and yours to keep.

Open Pressbooks Converter Example: Principles of Social Psychology

The origin story. I built the Pressbooks Converter first, to see what might be still missing from the Helios Open Reader for open textbook authors. The Common Cartridge Converter followed, doing the same for Helios Course Hub. Seeing they could also help others migrate their own content, I made them available here.
